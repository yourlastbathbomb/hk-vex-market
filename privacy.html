HK VEX Market: Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

HK VEX Market is a play-points prediction game for Hong Kong V5RC teams, run by a student for the teams' group chat. No money is involved and points have no cash value. This page says what the game stores about you.

What is stored

  • From Google, when you sign in: your Google account's ID number and your email address. They are used only to recognise you next time and to keep it to one account per person. The game never sees your Google password, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.
  • From you: the display name and team number you choose when you join.
  • From playing: your trades, positions and points.

Who can see it

  • Other players see your display name, team number, trades and points (in trade feeds and on the leaderboard).
  • Only the person who runs the game can see your email address and Google ID. They are never shown to other players.

Where it is kept

Everything is kept in a private Google Sheet owned by the person who runs the game. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything other than running the game. There are no ads and no analytics.

The page itself is hosted by GitHub, and it loads its fonts and the sign-in button from Google, so those companies receive the ordinary technical information any website visit sends (such as your IP address).

On your device

After you sign in, the page keeps a sign-in token in your browser's storage so you stay signed in for up to 30 days. Signing out removes it.

Removing your data

Ask an admin in the group chat and your account, including your email address and Google ID, will be deleted.
